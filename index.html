Preserving the photographic record of the sky

The International Glass Plates Group brings together people who preserve, study, digitize, and share historical astronomical glass plates.

About the group

Astronomical glass plates are historic records of the night sky. The IGPG is an informal international network for people and institutions interested in caring for these collections and making them accessible for research and public engagement.

Our focus

Preservation

Share approaches to caring for fragile plates and their associated records.

Digitization

Exchange ideas about practical ways to scan, describe, and provide access to collections.

Research and access

Support scientific, historical, and educational uses of astronomical plate archives.
